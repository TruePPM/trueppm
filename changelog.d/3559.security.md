Documented that one `INTEGRATION_ENCRYPTION_KEY` encrypts every stored third-party secret (the SSO client secret, per-user Jira credentials, outgoing webhook secrets, board-automation secrets, and the outbound SMTP password), and that a changed or lost key with no `TRUEPPM_INTEGRATION_ENCRYPTION_KEY_FALLBACKS` entry still strands every stored secret (recovery is to re-enter each one); zero-downtime rotation through the fallback list and `reencrypt_integration_secrets` is described under Rotating secrets without downtime.
