- **A task's baseline variance no longer reads a weekend hop as three days late or early (#4203).** Follow-up to #4197. The baseline chip on a board card and in the task drawer subtracted the shown dates on the client, and the task list's `schedule_variance_days` subtracted them on the server. A milestone baselined at the end of a Friday and now shown at the start of the following Monday therefore read +3d, and one baselined at the start of a Monday and completed on the Friday before read -3 on the task list while the program rollup's schedule variance read 0. Both are now measured in working time. The task payload gains `baseline_finish_variance_days`, the forecast finish against the active baseline, which the two chips now display. Monte Carlo deltas (`delta_vs_cpm`, added time `risk_premium_days`, and the history and what-if `p50`/`p80`/`p95` deltas) are measured in working time too, since #4204.
