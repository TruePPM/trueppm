- Six new webhook events (the OSS cap rises from 20 to 26 with ADR-1485 D6 and ADR-1477): `changeset.opened` (first structural change since a baseline), `changeset.entry_added` (each later changeset entry, once per entry rather than per act), `changeset.entry_reasoned` (an unreasoned entry is given its reason), `baseline.kept` (Keep v1, let variance stand, with `note`, `open_entries` and `variance_wd`), `schedule.change_undone` (a structural-operation undo, naming the reversed `event_id`), and `sprint.scope_change_requested`. Changeset entries carry `actor.agent_actor_id` and `actor.on_behalf_of` for an agent-made change. `sprint.scope_changed` now also fires on a rejection, on slip decisions, and when the system closes a pending slip (superseded, stale on accept, another slip accepted, sprint closed), with `status`, `kind`, the actors and a new `closed_by` (`person` or `system`; a system closure has `decided_by: null`), so every slip that sent `sprint.scope_change_requested` also gets a closing event. `baseline.kept` also carries `decision_id`, `baseline_id`, `from_baseline_id`, `baseline_version` and `actor`. `baseline.captured` from Take v2 gains `reason_code`, `reason`, `commitment_moved`, `changeset_id`, `decision_id` (the same id `baseline.kept` uses) and `from_baseline_id` (the baseline it replaced); `schedule.finish_changed` gains `finish_delta_working_days`. Payloads are ids and deltas only; `sprint.scope_changed` keeps its pre-existing `item_name`.
- A permanently failed webhook delivery can be listed (`GET .../deliveries/?status=failed`, from a session or a full-access token) and replayed (`POST .../webhooks/{id}/deliveries/{delivery_id}/replay/`, session or `webhooks:manage` token, refused for `api:full`). It keeps its `delivery_id` and sequence number, and replay stays available on a closed program's or an archived project's webhook. Failed deliveries are now retained at least 14 days regardless of `TRUEPPM_WEBHOOK_RETENTION_DAYS`.
