- Five new webhook events (the cap rises from 20 to 24 with ADR-1485 D6 and ADR-1477): `changeset.opened` (first structural change since a baseline), `baseline.kept` (Keep v1, let variance stand, with `note`, `open_entries` and `variance_wd`), `schedule.change_undone` (names the reversed `event_id`), and `sprint.scope_change_requested`. `sprint.scope_changed` now also fires on a rejection and on slip decisions, with `status`, `kind` and the actors. `baseline.captured` from Take v2 gains `reason_code`, `reason`, `commitment_moved` and `changeset_id`; `schedule.finish_changed` gains `finish_delta_working_days`. Payloads are ids and deltas only.
- A permanently failed webhook delivery can be listed (`GET .../deliveries/?status=failed`) and replayed (`POST .../webhooks/{id}/deliveries/{delivery_id}/replay/`, session or `webhooks:manage` token, refused for `api:full`). It keeps its `delivery_id` and sequence number. Failed deliveries are now retained at least 14 days regardless of `TRUEPPM_WEBHOOK_RETENTION_DAYS`.
