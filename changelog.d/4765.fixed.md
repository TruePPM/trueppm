The `--chrome-border` token ignored its alpha, so bare `border-chrome-border` hairlines (dialogs, popovers, bottom nav, top bar) rendered solid black or white instead of ~8%. Tailwind now falls back to `--chrome-border-opacity` when no `/N` modifier is given. The new `/8` opacity step also makes three `bg-brand-primary/8` tints paint that never rendered before: the selected row in the dead-letter inspector, the Schedule mode chip, and the selected option in the ceremony modal.
