- **Scheduler: a longer task no longer moves a milestone, or the project finish, a day earlier (#4173).** When a lag landed a milestone on a weekend or holiday, the engine showed it on the working day *before* the non-working days if the milestone followed a Finish-to-Start link, and on the working day *after* them if it followed a Start-to-Start link. So lengthening a predecessor could move a milestone and the project finish from Monday back to Friday, and Monte Carlo could forecast P50 and P80 earlier than the CPM finish. Such a milestone is now shown at the start of the next working day, the way MS Project places an elapsed lag that ends on non-working time. A milestone whose predecessor finishes on a working day is unchanged when it has no lag, or when its lag ends on a working day. A milestone right after a task whose recorded finish falls on a weekend now also shows at the start of the next working day, not on the Friday before that finish. The Python engine, the Rust/WASM engine and the client preview move together.
