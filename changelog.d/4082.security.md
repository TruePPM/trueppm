Project and program export no longer include a blocked task's private blocker reason (ADR-0124) for a requester who is neither the task's assignee nor @-mentioned on it, including project/program Admins. The JSON seed omits `reason`, and the async bundle blanks `blocked_reason` in `history/tasks.json`; type, age, and links are still exported. Re-importing such a seed marks the task blocked with the placeholder reason `(private)`.
