Scheduler: a data date or SNET (`planned_start`) at or before the project start no longer moves any work. A free or completed milestone used to measure its outgoing lags from that earlier day, and because a project with no status date uses today as its data date, every project starting in the future had its kickoff milestones' successors, forecast finish and critical path pulled up to (start - today) days early. This also applies to SF-linked tasks: a data date or SNET on or before the start no longer holds them. A data date or SNET after the start, and a recorded `actual_start`, bind as before. Both the Python and Rust/WASM engines changed.
