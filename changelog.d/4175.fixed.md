- **Monte Carlo matches the CPM finish when work was started on a weekend**: on a project with no uncertainty, `monte_carlo()` could report a finish 2-3 working days after `schedule()` when an in-progress task or a live milestone recorded an `actual_start` on a non-working day. The simulation snapped that date to the next working day, and a calendar-day lag measured from the snapped Monday instead of the recorded Sunday re-landed across a weekend. A live milestone floored at such a date, and an SS successor of work it starts, now resolve from the verbatim date as `schedule()` does, and a milestone held on its weekend actual can be the finish date. The error only ever ran late (MC over-reported risk). The `monte_carlo()` docstring's "at most one working day after" bound, which was false, is corrected (#4175).
