- **Monte Carlo finish after completed pre-start work**: a completed task whose
  last working day fell before the Monte Carlo index origin (before the project
  start, or before the first day its own calendar works on or after it) was read
  as finishing at the end of that first indexed day, so P50/P80/P95 could land up
  to several working days after `schedule()`'s finish on a deterministic project.
  Such a task now contributes only its recorded finish date, and an all-complete
  project reports its actual finish instead of its start date.
