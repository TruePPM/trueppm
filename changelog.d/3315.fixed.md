Applying a project template now refuses a structure whose tasks carry an unrecognized `delivery_mode`, `governance_class` or `type`, a milestone that disagrees with its delivery mode or has a nonzero duration, or a duration that is not a whole number of days, instead of writing the row.
Publishing or previewing a template from a project that holds an internally inconsistent task (a milestone flag that disagrees with its delivery mode, or a milestone with a non-zero duration) is now refused with the task named by title and WBS position, rather than by an opaque id. The validation deliberately does not require zero duration to mean milestone, because quick-capture items and phase rows are legitimately zero-duration.
