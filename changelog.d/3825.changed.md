The scheduling engine's `MAX_DEPENDENCIES` and `MAX_EXPANDED_EDGES` caps are raised from 100,000 to 250,000 (Python engine and WASM engine alike), so a 50,000-task network at two links per task no longer sits exactly on the limit. The engine measured 3.5 s and 357 MB at 100,000 tasks and 299,994 edges; worker memory in the API, not the engine, is the binding limit on very large plans.
