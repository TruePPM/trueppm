Editing a project's start date, status date or program, changing a planned sprint's start date, or deleting a sprint now recomputes the schedule, so dates, float and critical-path flags no longer stay stale until an unrelated edit. Tasks in a deleted sprint no longer stay pinned to that sprint's start date; deleting the sprint removes its start-date floor, and the schedule recomputes the task's dates as if it had no sprint. Closing a sprint while a schedule recompute is already pending no longer rolls the close back and leaves the sprint active.
