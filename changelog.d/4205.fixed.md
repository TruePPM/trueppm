- **Scheduling engine**: a milestone reached through a calendar-day lag could shift a
  successor's shown date by one working day compared to the equivalent direct
  dependency, and could distort the finish date and late dates of unrelated tasks
  through the shared project-finish seed — including in Monte Carlo. Inserting a
  milestone into a finish-to-start chain now leaves every other task's dates
  unchanged, as intended by #4079 (#4205).
