- **`perturb_task_duration` no longer completes a task, and what-if results change**: a pull-in that used up a task's remaining work used to record it as finished, cutting it off from its predecessors. The critical path fell to its successors, and the Monte Carlo bands could move further than the pull-in (P80 by 4 working days on a 1-day pull-in) because the predecessors' risk dropped out. A zero or negative delta on a started milestone did the same. The task now stays open at zero duration with its percent complete and actuals as they were, so its predecessors stay coupled and the finish and bands move by the days pulled in. This overturns the completion behavior introduced with the fix for #4432. A zero delta now returns every task unchanged.
