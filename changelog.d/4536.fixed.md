- **`perturb_task_duration` no longer completes a started milestone**: a what-if with a zero or negative delta on a zero-duration task carrying a `percent_complete` used to set `actual_finish`, move the finish earlier, and drop the task and its predecessors off the critical path. Its duration, percentage and actuals now stay as they were; on a negative delta its three-point estimate and story points still shift. A zero delta now returns every task unchanged.
