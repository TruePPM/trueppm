- **`perturb_task_duration` no longer completes a task, and what-if results change**: a pull-in that used up a task's remaining work used to record it as finished, cutting it off from its predecessors. The critical path fell to its successors, and the Monte Carlo bands could move further than the pull-in (P80 by 4 working days on a 1-day pull-in) because the predecessors' risk dropped out. A zero or negative delta on a started milestone did the same. The task now stays open with `Task.zero_remaining` set (#4819), its duration, percent complete and actuals as they were, so its predecessors stay coupled. With no lag on finish-to-start or finish-to-finish links out of it, the CPM finish moves by the days pulled in. This overturns the completion behavior introduced with the fix for #4432. A zero delta now returns every task unchanged. The what-if endpoint's `applied.new_duration_days` reports the perturbed task's new duration, and 0 for a task with no remaining work, whose forecast keeps its full duration with `Task.zero_remaining` set.
