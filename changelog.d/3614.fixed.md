- **Gantt legend no longer permanently occludes the timeline**: the floating legend on the Schedule view opened by default on every visit, so a 280×300px panel sat over the lower-right of a ~320px-wide timeline pane at 1280 indefinitely. It now opens on your first-ever visit and closes by default on every visit after, controlled by a new toolbar **Legend** toggle and a close control on the panel itself — both stay in sync and persist your choice across reloads.
