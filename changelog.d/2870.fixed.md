Annotated no-body `@action` endpoints (archive, close, activate, mark-all-read, the three suggestion accept/decline/revoke routes, and others) no longer publish a required full write-serializer request body in the OpenAPI schema. About 12 body-reading actions (sprint close, bulk scope-change accept/reject, task split, project and sponsorship transfer, reorder, and others) now declare the body they actually read; their fields were already required at runtime, but a generated client will have new required fields, so regenerate clients. A blank `estimate_status` on existing tasks is repaired by a data migration and no longer accepted. Dead permission kwargs on actions are removed.
