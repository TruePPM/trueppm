The Monte Carlo cap refusal (`POST /projects/{id}/monte-carlo/` and the what-if endpoint) now answers HTTP 400 with `{error: "simulation_cap_exceeded", max_runs, max_tasks, message}` instead of HTTP 402 with `"tier": "team"`. Integrators that branched on the 402 status or the `tier` key must branch on `code`. `MC_SIMULATION_CAP` and `MC_TASK_CAP` are now configurable with `TRUEPPM_MC_SIMULATION_CAP` and `TRUEPPM_MC_TASK_CAP` (Helm `env:`).
