- `GET /api/v1/sprints/{id}/commitment-status/` (experimental) lists the committed tasks the last recalculation places after the sprint's end, each with the server's one-line `cause` and a `derivation_ref`, and the scope changes (`injection` and `slip`) still awaiting a decision, with batch, requester, age and proposed dates. Any project member can read it; accept and reject keep the Admin-and-up gate. After every recalculation, and when a sprint is activated, tasks that have just become at risk send the new `sprint.commitment_at_risk` webhook once, including on projects that recalculate as part of their program. The MCP server exposes the read as `get_sprint_commitment_status`.
