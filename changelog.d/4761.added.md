People capacity view and Resource Manager home (shell v2 preview). The rail's **People** item now opens `/people` on a load-by-person-by-week heat map, with the resource catalog as its **Directory** tab, and the Resource Manager home (`home_override = "rm"`) shows the same **Team load** grid. Both read a new endpoint, `GET /api/v1/me/people-load/`: each person's weekly load (booked ÷ capacity, time off removed) summed across the projects where you hold the Scheduler role or above and the project's team has shared its capacity signal with your role band, with a definition, an `as_of`, a count of people over capacity, and a reason when a figure cannot be computed. Over-allocated cells and badges carry a ▲ as well as color, through shared `OverallocBadge` / `OverallocBanner` components the board card now uses too. The sprint capacity panel (`GET /sprints/{id}/capacity/`) and the My Work capacity card gain a definition and an `as_of` naming them as sprint-scoped figures.
