Scheduler: closing work recorded as finishing after an already-closed milestone's sign-off day no longer moves that milestone's successors. The engine used to read any completed predecessor finishing on or after the recorded day as placing the milestone at the end of that day, so closing out-of-sequence work slipped the gate's successors, and possibly the project finish, by a working day. Now only completed work that places the milestone on its recorded day gives it the end of the day, as the documentation states. Both the Python and the Rust/WASM engines apply the rule. A milestone closed before its predecessors' recorded finish now sits at the start of its day, where it was while that work was open (#4703).
