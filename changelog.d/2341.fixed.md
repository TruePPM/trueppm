A collaborator's plain field edit (name, notes, labels, custom fields) now refreshes one task row in your open Schedule or board with a single `GET /tasks/{id}/` instead of re-downloading every page of the task list. Edits to status, progress, duration, dates, parent, order, sprint, dependency, owner or assignee (#4950), events without a field list, or a failed read still fall back to the full refetch.
