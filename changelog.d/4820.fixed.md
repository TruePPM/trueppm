- **A what-if pull-in no longer moves a sprint-delivered task's Monte Carlo bands later**: when a pull-in used up the remaining work of a started velocity-sampled task, a band could step backward as the pull-in grew (P80 from 10 working days earlier to 6 on a 10-day task at 40% with 40 points) or land later than the current forecast. A bigger pull-in no longer lands a band later than the last pull-in that leaves work open, across the sampled runs and in the seeded output for the default velocity settings in the tested range. Not covered: a task with a three-point estimate whose points reach zero ([#4909](https://gitlab.com/trueppm/trueppm/-/issues/4909)), and a what-if that changes the sampled-run layout under other velocity histories or sprint lengths ([#4910](https://gitlab.com/trueppm/trueppm/-/issues/4910)). At the point the remaining work runs out, the bands can move further than the days pulled in, because the what-if removes enough story points to hold even if the team delivers its fastest sprint.
