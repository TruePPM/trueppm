- **A what-if pull-in no longer moves a sprint-delivered task's Monte Carlo bands later**: when a pull-in used up the remaining work of a started velocity-sampled task, a band could step backward as the pull-in grew (P80 from 10 working days earlier to 6 on a 10-day task at 40% with 40 points) or land later than the current forecast. Every band is now non-increasing as the pull-in grows. At the point the remaining work runs out, the bands can move further than the days pulled in, because the what-if removes enough story points to hold even if the team delivers its fastest sprint.
