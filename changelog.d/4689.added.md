Scheduler self-check: after every schedule recalculation the worker checks the result against engine invariants (no negative float, critical exactly when float is zero, the finish on the critical path, a repeat recalculation gives the same result, and a zero-variance Monte Carlo lands on the critical-path finish). Violations are counted on the new `trueppm.scheduler.selfcheck.violations` OpenTelemetry counter and logged; the check never blocks a recalculation. The two expensive checks are sampled under a time budget, and projects above 2,000 tasks are skipped. Turn it off with `TRUEPPM_SCHEDULER_SELFCHECK_ENABLED=false`. A new Scheduler-only endpoint, `GET /api/v1/projects/<id>/schedule/bug-report-export/`, downloads the project's engine input with names stripped, plus the self-check verdict and engine version, to attach to a scheduling bug report.
