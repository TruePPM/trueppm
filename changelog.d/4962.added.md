- **Extension signal when a webhook auto-deactivates (api)**: `webhook_auto_deactivated` (`trueppm_api.apps.webhooks.signals`) is sent after commit, through `dispatch_extension_signal`, when the failure guard deactivates a webhook. Payload: `webhook_id`, `project_id` or `program_id`, `reason`, `failure_count`, `deactivated_at`. It carries no URL or secret, and a receiver's exception cannot fail the delivery task.
