A Start-to-Start or Start-to-Finish link out of a summary task can no longer reach the database through a path other than the dependency editor. Indent, outdent, and reparent now refuse with a 400 (`code: "summary_start_link"`), and creating a task under a parent with a 400 on `parent_id`, naming the links in the way, when the move would make such a task a summary, instead of succeeding and leaving the project's schedule failing every recalculation. MS Project, CSV/Excel, and Jira imports and template apply skip such a link and report it in the import's warnings while the rest of the import lands (seed loads skip it with a logged warning), and undoing an ungroup no longer revives one. The CSV import result also keeps the warnings its shared persistence step records, which it previously discarded.
