`GET /api/v1/projects/{id}/cost/` now reports CPI, EAC (typical-variance), ETC, VAC and TCPI, each as `{available, value | reason}` with new reasons `no_actual_cost`, `no_earned_value` and `budget_exhausted` instead of a zero or infinite value, and a `provenance` block naming the computing function, contract version, rate source, percent-complete basis and EAC method, with each figure marked computed or restricted. The `budget` and `budget_utilization` definitions now say that budget is BAC and that utilization is not a CPI. (#4852, #4848)

The forecast formulas are the commonly published ones and are not yet confirmed against the PMI standard (ADR-1324 addendum). Rate-change history is not recorded, because rates have no history. The program rollup cost KPIs do not carry the `provenance` block: they already report `available`/`reason` and counts per KPI.
