- **Scheduler: a finish-to-finish link out of a milestone now measures from the milestone itself**: an FF link out of a milestone at the start of a working day measured from the end of the working day before it, so a weekend could absorb a lag on that link or on a link after the next milestone. A task could start a working day earlier than the same finish-to-start link allows. The link now measures from the milestone's own instant (#4273).
