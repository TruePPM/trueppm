**Scheduler:** a task with `percent_complete` but no `actual_start` no longer moves its start-to-start or start-to-finish successors, or the project finish, earlier when its duration grows. Those links now measure from where the task's remaining work resumes (`early_start`) instead of the span start its elapsed days imply, in `schedule()`, `monte_carlo()`, the WASM engine and the Gantt drag preview; the bar (`scheduled_start`) still shows the derived span. Record `actual_start` to measure the links from when the work began. Seeded results on such projects can change, and the 0.5.0-dev result-stability stamp is re-stamped for it. The private `trueppm_scheduler.engine` helpers `_elapsed_link_days`, `_window_start_bound` and `_mc_span_link` are removed (#5182).
