**The development compose stack now generates its own `SECRET_KEY` on first boot** (#4672). `docker-compose.yml` used to ship the key `dev-secret-key-change-in-prod`, published in the repository, for Django sessions and CSRF signing. A one-shot `secrets-init` service now writes a random key to a `dev_secrets` volume, shared by the api, celery and celery-beat services, so no two stacks share a key and a restart keeps it. `docker compose down -v` discards it, which signs everyone out. Set `SECRET_KEY` in your environment to supply your own.
