**Scheduler:** a milestone with nothing before it, held by an SNET (`planned_start`), the data date or a recorded `actual_start`, now links its successors from the earliest midnight that is the same working-time point as that floor (Saturday 00:00 for a Monday on a Monday-to-Friday week), as a floored milestone with a predecessor already did. Pinning a start-of-day milestone on its shown late start no longer moves the project finish, and adding a predecessor that does not hold a milestone no longer moves its lagged successors. A calendar-day lag out of such a milestone now counts from that Saturday, so `M -FS+3cd-> B` with `M` held on Monday starts `B` on Tuesday, not Thursday. An SS or SF lag out of such a milestone counts from that Saturday too, so it can bound its successor up to two calendar days earlier than the same link out of work starting that Monday. A milestone marked complete with no actuals, held by its SNET, links from the same midnight it did live, so closing it that way no longer moves its lagged successors. The Rust/WASM engine and Monte Carlo apply the same rule (#5094).
