**Scheduler:** a milestone with nothing before it, held by an SNET (`planned_start`), the data date or a recorded `actual_start`, now links its successors from the earliest midnight that is the same working-time point as that floor (Saturday 00:00 for a Monday on a Monday-to-Friday week), as a floored milestone with a predecessor already did. Pinning a start-of-day milestone on its shown late start no longer moves the project finish, and adding a predecessor that does not hold a milestone no longer moves its lagged successors. A calendar-day lag out of such a milestone now counts from that Saturday, so `M -FS+3cd-> B` with `M` held on Monday starts `B` on Tuesday, not Thursday. The Rust/WASM engine and Monte Carlo apply the same rule (#5094).
