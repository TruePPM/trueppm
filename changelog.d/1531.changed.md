- **CPM duration layout is arithmetic instead of a day-by-day calendar walk (scheduler)**: `schedule()` laid every task's duration out by stepping one working day at a time, so the passes cost time in proportion to the sum of all durations. The working day a duration lands on is now computed by rank arithmetic over the weekday mask and the calendar's exception prefix sum, falling back to the walk only where the walk would raise. A 5,000-task schedule runs about 25-30% faster on the same host. Results are unchanged, including the errors raised for degenerate calendars; a differential test checks the new layout against the old walk.
