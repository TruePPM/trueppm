- **A new user's first project opens with a starter rail of about six views instead of fourteen**: creating your first project seeds your personal hidden-views preference from the methodology you chose (Hybrid starts with Dashboard, Schedule, Grid, Board, Today and Settings). Dashboard and Settings are never hidden, the set is yours to edit from **Customize views**, and later projects or an existing customized rail are never touched. Changing a project's methodology later does not re-seed.
