Monte Carlo velocity forecasting (experimental) now draws one shared team capacity per run: every SCRUM task with story points consumes the same per-sprint velocity sequence in dependency order, finishes part-way through a sprint instead of being rounded up to whole sprints, and an in-progress story needs only its remaining points (`story_points * (1 - percent_complete / 100)`). Ten sequential 2-point stories at 20 points a sprint now forecast one sprint instead of ten, and five parallel 20-point stories five sprints instead of one (ADR-1412; #5011, #5012).
