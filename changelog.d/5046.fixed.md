- **Committing a backlog, epic or recurring subtask under an FF/SF target**: changing
  a task's status or type, or attaching or removing a recurrence rule, so that the
  scheduler starts (or stops) scheduling it is now refused with a 400 when it would
  make every later recalculation fail — most often a Finish-to-Finish or
  Start-to-Finish link into a parent whose only subtasks were in the backlog, which
  becomes a summary the moment one of them commits. Creating or restoring a scheduled
  subtask under such a parent is refused the same way (every row of a `tasks/bulk`
  batch is checked, not only the first under each parent), a mobile sync upload
  reports the row as a conflict and commits the rest of the batch, and inbound task
  sync keeps the old status (or leaves the task unattached) with a warning. The check
  also models the phase status rollup, so a parent the rollup had parked in the
  backlog is caught too. Still unchecked, and tracked in #5046: returning tasks to the
  backlog when a sprint closes, deleting a task, program-wide recalculation, and links
  from another project. The seed importer's missing type-aware check is #5154.
