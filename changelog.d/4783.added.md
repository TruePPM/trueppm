The weekly timesheet now limits its rows to open tasks scheduled during the week you're viewing, instead of putting every open assignment in every week. A task counts when its scheduled start-to-finish span overlaps the week. An open task that's past its scheduled finish stays a row in the current week, and an open task with no scheduled dates is a row in every week, so late and unscheduled work stays loggable. **Add project or task** offers your other open assignments, such as one scheduled for a later week. Completed tasks you haven't logged time on that week are no longer offered there; use the quick-log popover for those. For contributors with a long history of completed work, the grid no longer misses upcoming open tasks. `GET /api/v1/me/work/` rows now carry `scheduled_start` and `scheduled_finish`, and the endpoint accepts `?open=1` to leave out completed tasks. The filter only narrows the caller's own list.
