- **⌘K "Log time on <last task>" quick action**: after you open a task, the command palette offers a **Log time on *task name*** action (Member and above on that project). Choosing it swaps the palette to a one-field form — type minutes (`45`), hours (`1.5`) or `1:30` and press Enter to log against today without leaving the palette; a refused entry shows the server's reason inline. The last task is remembered per user in the browser.
