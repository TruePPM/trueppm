A failed workflow step is now retried and observable instead of stranding the workflow permanently (#2994). A transient step failure is re-queued by the outbox drain (up to three attempts, 60 s apart) before the workflow is rolled back and marked failed; a step that raises `NonRetryableError` fails at once; an orphaned step that exhausts its attempts is abandoned into `failed` rather than left wedged. The retention purge now spares rows still waiting on a retry. New `GET /api/v1/admin/workflows/` and `/{id}/` (workspace operators) show which workflow failed, at which step, and why. The dead-letter requeue no longer stamps the task `retried` before the requeue has actually run, and re-requeueing a failed requeue now re-runs it. A dropped dead letter is no longer re-dispatched by a requeue that was still waiting, a repeated **Requeue all** skips tasks whose requeue is already in flight, and the `trueppm.outbox.depth` gauge reports a `retrying` state for workflow steps waiting on a retry. A requeue POSTed to an older API pod during a rolling update is still carried out, a failure to stamp the task `retried` no longer re-dispatches it again, and a custom `WORKFLOW_BACKEND` written before the restart option keeps accepting requeues (it logs a warning once and starts without it). Requeuing a failed workflow resumes it at the failed step, with completed steps served from cache rather than run again; a workflow whose rollback compensated a step is refused a restart, and that refusal still holds after its history has been purged.
