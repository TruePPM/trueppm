A failed workflow step is now retried and observable instead of stranding the workflow permanently (#2994). A transient step failure is re-queued by the outbox drain (up to three attempts, 60 s apart) before the workflow is rolled back and marked failed; a step that raises `NonRetryableError` fails at once; an orphaned step that exhausts its attempts is abandoned into `failed` rather than left wedged. The retention purge now spares rows still waiting on a retry. New `GET /api/v1/admin/workflows/` and `/{id}/` (workspace operators) show which workflow failed, at which step, and why. The dead-letter requeue no longer stamps the task `retried` before the requeue has actually run, and re-requeueing a failed requeue now re-runs it.
