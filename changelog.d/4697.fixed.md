- **Monte Carlo shows a fractional finish on the day it ends in** (#4697): before,
  each run's finish was rounded to the nearest day, half to even. A run whose work
  ended 0.3 of the way into Monday counted as finishing Friday, so P50 and P80 could
  equal the CPM finish when no run finished there. A 10-day task estimated 10/10/11
  is the example. Links, lags and milestones now read the fraction the same way. A
  run with no spread still lands exactly on the CPM finish. A zero-duration task
  with an estimate is exempt: a draw under half a day still rounds away to the CPM
  instant and hands nothing on, so a 0/0/1 estimate keeps P50 on the CPM finish.
  **Seeded P50/P80/P95 move.** On networks built from FS and SS links they never
  move earlier and usually move one working day later. Networks with lagged links
  can move further, up to four working days in 600 seeded random networks, because
  the nearest-day reading could lose a day at each lagged link. On networks with FF
  or SF links a percentile can also move earlier. Forecast history shows a one-time
  step at the upgrade.
