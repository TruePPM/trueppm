The Schedule's "What moved and why" panel no longer disappears for the largest recalculations. When a CPM run changes more tasks than the live update can itemize, the panel now says how many tasks had their dates or float changed and that they are too many to list, instead of rendering nothing — and it re-opens for such a run even if you dismissed it earlier. Screen readers hear the same once, through the schedule's change announcement. No per-task rows or causes are invented for such a run.
