Seed export now writes a task's `actual_start` and `actual_finish`, and the importer reads them back, so a project re-imported from its own export keeps its in-flight schedule floor. Seed validation now applies the shared actual-date rules: it refuses a malformed or out-of-range actual date, an `actual_finish` earlier than `actual_start`, and an `actual_finish` on a task that is not in review or complete.
