- **Scheduler: recording a milestone on the day it is shown no longer moves its successors** (#4690): marking a milestone done or started on the day the schedule showed it, or setting its `planned_start` to that day, could push its lagged successors and the project finish one or two working days later. This happened when the milestone sat at a midnight before the start of its shown day, such as Sunday 00:00 shown on Monday, or a point before the project start. A closed milestone now links from the midnight it linked from live, read from its completed predecessors. A recorded `actual_start` or `planned_start` on the shown day, or a data date that falls on it, now leaves its successors where they were. One case still differs: closed while a predecessor is still open, the milestone links from the close of the working day before its recorded day. Both the Python and the Rust/WASM engines are fixed.
