Scheduler: a finish-to-finish successor of completed work recorded as finished on a non-working day (a Saturday or Sunday `actual_finish`) now finishes where a Friday finish puts it, not on the Monday after. Both engines read a non-working `actual_finish` as the end of the last working day before it. A zero-lag finish-to-start milestone after a Saturday or Sunday `actual_finish` now shows at the end of Friday (`milestone_at_day_end` true) instead of the start of Monday, and recalculating an existing project after upgrade moves such successors.
