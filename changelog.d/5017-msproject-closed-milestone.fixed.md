**MS Project import: a milestone closed at the end of its day keeps that edge.** A completed milestone whose `<ActualFinish>` time is at or after the end of its working day (the shift end its calendar states for that weekday, else the file's `<DefaultFinishTime>`, else 17:00) is now imported at the end of its day, so its finish-to-start successors start the next working day, as in the file. A TruePPM export, which writes such a milestone at its calendar's shift end, re-imports with the edge kept, and the export now also writes `<DefaultFinishTime>`. Since a signed-off milestone's edge is decided only by the previous run's hand-back (#5017), an imported plan has no previous run, and without this such a milestone recomputed at the start of its day and pulled its successors a working day earlier ([#5017](https://gitlab.com/trueppm/trueppm/-/issues/5017)).
