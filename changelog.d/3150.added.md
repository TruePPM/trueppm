Committed plans now keep a baseline changeset: every structural edit after baseline v1 (add, move or delete) joins it with an optional, set-once reason, and the plan is reviewed with two recorded exits, take a new baseline or keep the current one and let variance stand. New endpoints: `GET /projects/{id}/changeset/`, `PATCH /projects/{id}/changeset/entries/{id}/`, and `GET`/`POST /projects/{id}/baseline-decisions/` with a `preview/`. MCP gains the `get_changeset` and `list_baseline_decisions` read tools. In the app, the project header and the Schedule view show the open changes with a Review action, a non-modal reason line under the Schedule toolbar asks why after a structural edit (Alt+W reaches it), and the review sheet lists unanswered changes first with the two exits as equal choices, including on a phone.
