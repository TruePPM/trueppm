Committed plans now keep a baseline changeset: every structural edit after baseline v1 (add, move or delete) joins it with an optional, set-once reason, and the plan is reviewed with two recorded exits, take a new baseline or keep the current one and let variance stand. New endpoints: `GET /projects/{id}/changeset/`, `PATCH /projects/{id}/changeset/entries/{id}/`, and `GET`/`POST /projects/{id}/baseline-decisions/` with a `preview/`. MCP gains the `get_changeset` and `list_baseline_decisions` read tools.
