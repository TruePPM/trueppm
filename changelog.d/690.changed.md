Tasks now have a soft **deadline**: a committed date that the scheduler does not read or enforce. Set it in the task drawer below the schedule strip. When the forecast finish lands after it, the drawer shows "N days past deadline". The field is `deadline` on the task REST API and in the sync delta, and MS Project `<Deadline>` now imports and exports instead of being reported as dropped. **Webhook behavior change:** `task.due_date_changed` now fires only when a task's `deadline` changes. It previously fired on `planned_start` changes. Its payload carries `deadline` and `previous_deadline` in place of `previous_planned_start`. The in-app and email notification of the same name still fires on a `planned_start` change, and now on a `deadline` change too.
