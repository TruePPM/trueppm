Link creation now enforces the workspace/program/project "Public sharing" policy (ADR-0135) inside the share-link mint routine itself, including the pinned-token path of `create_demo_share_link`, so no creation path (the share endpoint, a management command, the sample-data loader, or a future bulk or automation path) can mint a public link once the policy is seen to be off. The share endpoint keeps its 403 when the policy flips off between its own check and the mint; a flip after the service's check but before the insert can still mint a link, which is harmless because serving re-checks and answers 410. Behavior change: the sample-data loader now skips the sample share link (Aurora) when Public sharing is off, which is the default; turn it on in Workspace Settings before loading to get the link.
