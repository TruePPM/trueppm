- **Helm: explicit `0`/`false` overrides are no longer clobbered back to the chart default**. Sprig's `| default` treats `0` and `false` as unset, so setting `replicas.web: 0`, `podDisruptionBudget.{api,worker,web}.maxUnavailable: 0`, `observability.otlp.{enabled,tracesEnabled,metricsEnabled}: false`, or `demo.backoffLimit: 0` silently rendered the opposite of what was configured. These four values — and two related alert thresholds, `alerts.thresholds.outboxDepth` and `alerts.thresholds.outboxOldestAgeSeconds` — now render the explicit value.
