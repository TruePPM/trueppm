Shell v2 preview (`?ff=shell_v2`): the Contributor, PM and Executive homes. The contributor home leads with your assigned work and none of the schedule vocabulary; the PM home shows the program's P80 finish with its curve, the biggest slip, what is blocked, decisions waiting and the projects; the executive home shows the same for one program at a time with a switcher.
