- **Session-ended prompt no longer claims your session "expired" when it didn't**: the modal and read-only banner shown when you're signed out mid-session said "Your session expired" even when the real cause was a password reset, an admin-initiated sign-out, or account deactivation. The title and banner copy are now cause-agnostic ("You've been signed out"), and the body explains that the session was ended rather than naming expiry specifically (#4256).
