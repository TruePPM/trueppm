Export bundles now carry an integrity manifest: a SHA-256 per archive member plus a `manifest_sha256` over the canonicalized manifest, the whole-artifact digest on the export job (`file_sha256`) and the download `X-Export-SHA256` header, and bundle uploads to the program import endpoint are verified before anything is written (mismatch returns 400; bundles without digests still import). The whole-artifact digest identifies one specific file; it is not stable across rebuilds of identical data (gzip and tar timestamps, `generated_at`), while per-member digests are. Verification bounds decompressed size and member count.
