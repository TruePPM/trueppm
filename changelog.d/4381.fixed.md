Celery task loops that isolate per-item failures with `except Exception` no longer swallow `SoftTimeLimitExceeded`: the signal now propagates and stops the task. The sprint-burndown, recurring-occurrence, daily-forecast-floor and digest sweeps process items in a fixed order and log a WARNING naming the items they did not reach; the other drains re-raise without the WARNING. A test now fails on any new unguarded per-item handler in a loop under `trueppm_api`.
