- **`max_lag_delta_cells` and `max_index_cells` are provisional (scheduler)**: `monte_carlo(max_lag_delta_cells=…)` with `MAX_LAG_DELTA_CELLS`, and `monte_carlo(max_index_cells=…)` with `MAX_INDEX_CELLS`, are counted in the cells of the current Monte Carlo implementation's lag-delta table and working-day indices. They stay exported, because a request-scoped embedder needs them to bound memory, but are now documented as exempt from the 1.x signature freeze: a release may rename them or replace them with an implementation-neutral budget, and will say so in the changelog. Nothing changes for callers today.
