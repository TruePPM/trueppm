Scheduler: a completed milestone that follows work and was closed on a day its own calendar does not work (a Saturday on a Monday-to-Friday calendar, for example) is now shown at the end of that day, as the scheduling conventions describe. It was shown at the start of the day while the project finish was measured from its end, so other work could report float that, once spent, moved the shown finish. On the run that closes it, its successors do not move. Because the end-of-day edge is now handed back to the next run, reopening work behind the milestone no longer pulls its successors a day earlier. A project whose finish is such a milestone may report a one-time 1-working-day finish shift on its next recalculation, in the end-date shift notification, the activity feed and baseline drift: the finish was stored as the start of that day and is now read as its end.
