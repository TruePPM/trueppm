Monte Carlo now reads a project that finishes on a completed milestone at the same edge of the day as the CPM finish. A closed milestone with no predecessor, or one placed after work that finished on an earlier day, sits at the start of its day, and a deterministic project's P50/P80/P95 previously read it as the end of that day, one working day after the CPM finish. The duration-sensitivity index of tasks in such a project can change too, because runs are now ranked by where the closed milestone sits in working time.
