Optional connection-pooling path: a `pgbouncer` Helm block renders a transaction-pooling PgBouncer in front of an external PostgreSQL, `TRUEPPM_DB_TRANSACTION_POOLING` turns off server-side cursors and enables connection health checks, and `DATABASE_DIRECT_URL` lets `migrate_locked` hold its advisory lock on the real database. The metrics exporter's `statement_timeout` is now transaction-local so it cannot leak across pooled sessions. The sizing guide states the connections-per-replica math.
