- **Fractional percent complete no longer schedules a day off** ([#5034](https://gitlab.com/trueppm/trueppm/-/issues/5034), [#5015](https://gitlab.com/trueppm/trueppm/-/issues/5015)):
  an in-progress task's elapsed days were float-truncated, so a 375-day task at
  18.4% counted 68 elapsed days instead of 69 and finished a working day late.
  The scheduler now computes `duration × percent / 100` exactly on the decimal
  the percentage was entered as, counts a whole day when the result is within
  1e-9 days of one (so a percent stored as 1 of 9 days, `11.11111111111111`,
  counts that day) and otherwise floors it, in the CPM pass, Monte Carlo, what-if
  shifts and the API's `remaining_duration`, which now matches the scheduled
  dates. An in-progress task always keeps at least one remaining day. The Rust/WASM engine applies
  the same rule and now parses percentages to the same value as Python, so a
  value such as `99.99999999999999` no longer reads as complete there.
  Whole-number percents schedule exactly as before. The Rust/WASM bundle grows
  by 31,914 bytes (12,545 gzip); it ships in no client today.
