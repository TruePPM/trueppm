**Scheduler:** a milestone closed while one of its predecessors is still open no longer moves when that open work's forecast changes, and closing it on its forecast day still moves nothing. The engine now decides a closed milestone's day edge from completed predecessors plus the edge the last recalculation recorded for it, never from open work's current forecast. Previously a longer predecessor could finish an FS-only schedule earlier, and the open predecessor's float was overstated. This was a regression in unreleased 0.4 work from the #4433 fix. Library callers can pass the new optional `Task.live_day_end_on` to get the same behavior. (#4525)
