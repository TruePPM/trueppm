**Scheduler:** a milestone closed while one of its predecessors is still open no longer moves when that open work's forecast changes. Only completed predecessors decide which edge of its recorded day a closed milestone sits on, so a longer predecessor can no longer finish an FS-only schedule earlier, and the open predecessor's float is no longer overstated. This was a regression in unreleased 0.4 work from the #4433 fix. (#4525)
