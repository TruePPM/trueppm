Cost readers: /kaizen's per-MR cost now sums each MR's windowed `## Cost of this MR` records (new `scripts/mr-cost-means.py`) instead of averaging the overlapping `## Gates` snapshot. An MR with no records, a legacy or unparsed record, a note by another user, record numbers not 1..n, a duplicate record or identical window, or an `n/a` cost is excluded with a printed count and reason, never read as 0.
