- **Scheduler: slipping a predecessor no longer moves a floored milestone's successors earlier** (#4883). A milestone held by an SNET (`planned_start`), the data date or a recorded `actual_start` on a working day linked its successors from the floor itself, unless its link already showed it at the start of that day, in which case they counted from the link's own earlier midnight (#4690). A predecessor slipping into that tie made the link instant jump back, from Monday 00:00 to Saturday or Sunday 00:00, so a successor on a wider calendar or behind a calendar-day lag moved earlier by as many calendar days as the non-working time before the floor (two across a weekend). A floored milestone now links from the later of its link and the close of the last working day before the floor, which never moves backward. **This changes live schedules:** a successor on a seven-day calendar can now sit on the weekend before a Monday-floored Monday-to-Friday milestone, and a calendar-day lag out of such a milestone counts from that Saturday (`M -FS+2cd-> B` with `M` held at Monday puts `B` on Monday, not Wednesday). A floor on a non-working day (only a verbatim weekend `actual_start` can be one) is read back to the start of its non-working run, so recording a milestone's start on Monday instead of Sunday can no longer move its successors earlier; the cost is that a Sunday `actual_start` now also links from the Saturday before, so its seven-day successors sit on that Saturday and a calendar-day lag out of it counts from Saturday, a day earlier than before. A lag out of such a milestone can therefore count from before its recorded `actual_start`, and before the project start. A signed-off (completed) milestone's successors can likewise sit on the non-working days before its recorded date (a seven-day successor of a milestone completed Monday sits on the Saturday or Sunday before it). Monotonicity holds for an SNET, the data date and a recorded `actual_start`; the project-start floor is excluded, so moving the project start later can still move a floored milestone's successors earlier (#5055). A floor with no non-working time before it, a Saturday `actual_start`, and a milestone with no predecessor are unchanged. The Python engine, its Monte Carlo pass and the Rust/WASM engine are all changed, and the reference oracle with them (ADR-0136, 2026-11-16 amendment).
