The per-user limit on concurrent offline-sync upload batches (`TRUEPPM_SYNC_MAX_CONCURRENT_BATCHES`) now gives each batch its own lease instead of sharing one expiring counter. A batch that runs past `TRUEPPM_SYNC_INFLIGHT_TTL_SECONDS` can no longer push the counter below zero and raise the limit for everyone afterwards. A slot left behind by a crashed worker now frees itself when its own lease ends, even while the user keeps uploading.
