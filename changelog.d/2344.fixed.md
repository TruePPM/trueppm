The product backlog grooming endpoint (`GET /projects/{id}/product-backlog/`) now serializes one rank-ordered page of stories (`limit`, default 500, max 2000; `offset` for later pages) and returns `truncated`, `limit` and `offset` fields; health and epic rollups still cover the whole backlog and are computed without loading every story. The grooming page (desktop and phone) and the sprint story picker show a "Showing the top N of M stories by rank" notice with a **Load next** button, so stories ranked past the first page stay reachable.
