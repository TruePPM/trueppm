- **Time tracking write paths now re-check live access**: logging time on a task,
  editing or deleting your own time entry, and starting or stopping a timer all
  now enforce the archived-project read-only rule and re-check that you still
  hold at least Team Member role on the project — not just at the moment the
  entry or timer was created. A member whose project access was removed or
  demoted can no longer write against it through these paths. If your project
  membership ends while a timer is running, that timer is discarded rather than
  auto-logged — including when the discard happens as the side effect of
  starting a new timer elsewhere.
