Resizing a task across a holiday no longer ends it a working day past the handle. The `duration` derived from a task write's `planned_finish` is now counted on the project's composed calendar — inherited base calendar, layered calendars and their holidays — rather than on its own calendar's weekly pattern alone, so the task ends on the date given (or, when that date is a non-working day, on the working day before it). The keyboard reschedule nudge also steps over holidays and onto working weekend days (#4170).
