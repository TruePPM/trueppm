- **Velocity and burndown over MCP, and the release forecast on the Product Backlog.** Two new read-only MCP tools: `get_sprint_velocity` returns the team's last closed sprints (committed and completed points and task counts), the rolling average and the forecast range, and `get_sprint_burndown` returns a sprint's daily burn series and pace verdict. Velocity is rendered through the team-private privacy gate, so a token whose owner is outside the team's velocity audience, which a project Admin or Owner is by default, gets `velocity_suppressed: true` and no figures rather than an error. `GET /projects/{id}/velocity/` now accepts `mcp:read` tokens and carries a `_provenance` stamp. The Product Backlog shows the backlog delivery forecast (P50 and P80 dates and sprint counts) beneath the grooming-health strip, the same answer the `get_release_forecast` tool returns. (#2411)
