- **The project history and changelog no longer send record ids to readers below Admin.** Fields such as `archived_by`, `deleted_by`, `accepted_by`, `milestone_bound_by` and `assignee` carried account ids to Members and Viewers, who are not shown who made a change. Every relation field's `old` and `new` are now `null` for those readers, with `old_display` / `new_display` set to `Hidden` where a value existed. Admins and Owners see the ids as before. (#5083)
