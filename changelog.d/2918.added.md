The New task dialog accepts an optional three-point (optimistic / most likely / pessimistic) estimate, sent all-or-none with the create request. The fields are hidden on agile projects, where estimates are story points.

Pasting rows from a spreadsheet also accepts Optimistic, Most Likely and Pessimistic columns, creating each row with its three-point estimate. A row whose triple is partial or out of order is created without one, and the paste receipt says how many rows that affected.
