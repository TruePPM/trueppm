The bundled Grafana dashboard's outbox-depth and DB-connections panels, and the `TruePPMOutboxDepthRising` alert, now aggregate with `max by (...)` instead of `sum by (...)`. Every TruePPM process (web, worker, beat) emits the same cluster-wide backlog and connection-count figures as its own series, so `sum` was multiplying the real value by the number of running processes — the alert could fire at roughly 10x actual outbox depth.
