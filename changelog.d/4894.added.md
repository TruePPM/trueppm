- **`trueppm-scheduler`: rendering-neutral graph export** — `to_graph(result, project)` returns a `GraphExport` of nodes (early/late dates, total float, critical flag) and edges (type, lag, `is_driving`) that round-trips through JSON (`to_json` / `from_json`) under a versioned format (`GRAPH_FORMAT_VERSION`). A cyclic or malformed graph raises and names the offending ids. The `DrivingEdge` field set is now a documented, frozen public contract. No scheduling semantics change.
