- **Scheduler: a milestone's late start is shown where its float stops when a milestone after it works a shorter week** ([#5092](https://gitlab.com/trueppm/trueppm/-/issues/5092)): this completes the inherited zero-lag cap of [#4526](https://gitlab.com/trueppm/trueppm/-/issues/4526) in the same release. `trueppm-scheduler` now shows a start-of-day milestone's `late_start` and `late_finish` on the day its `total_float` actually runs to. When a milestone linked after it with no lag is on a narrower calendar (Monday to Thursday, say, beside a Friday finish), the float stops where that milestone would be shown past `project_finish`, and the late date is now shown there too, not one working day later where holding the milestone moves the finish. Against 0.4, both `total_float` and the late start of such a milestone drop by one working day. The late-start derivation and the Rust/WASM engine show the same day. The conformance snapshots `zero_lag_milestone_chain_into_a_four_day_week` and `zero_lag_ss_chain_into_a_closed_week` move by one day, from Friday 2026-01-09 to Thursday 2026-01-08, and the 0.5.0-dev result-stability stamp is re-blessed for those two late dates.
