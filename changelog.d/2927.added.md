- Workspace Owners and Admins can restore access for a member who has no password — such as an account created by single sign-on whose provider was removed — by emailing them a one-time, 30-minute set-password link (`POST /api/v1/workspace/members/{id}/send-set-password-link/`). The link goes only to the member's own address and is never shown to the administrator; it is refused for API tokens, for members whose role is not lower than the caller's, and for members who already have a password. Every send attempt, including one whose email fails, is recorded in the audit log as `member_set_password_link_sent`, and each administrator is limited to 10 links per hour. (#2927)
