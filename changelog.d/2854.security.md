- **Per-person sprint capacity is now a governed team signal (api, web)**: `GET /sprints/{id}/capacity/` returned every assigned member's name with committed and available hours to every project member, the PM/Admin band included. It is now the `capacity` signal under ADR-0104, default `TEAM`/`TEAM`. The team band (Members, Viewers, Schedulers and the Scrum Master) still reads the per-person list by design. The PM/Admin band is suppressed by default: it gets `members: []` and `capacity_suppressed: true` while the aggregate `totals` stay, and the over-capacity `warnings` on `POST /sprints/{id}/activate/` are reduced to `{"type": "over_capacity", "detail_suppressed": true}` with no names or hours. The team can share the signal upward from Settings → Signal privacy, which now lists Capacity and Flow metrics. A new completeness test fails when a serializer in `apps/projects/serializers.py` that carries `member_id`/`member_name` is not registered to a governed signal.
