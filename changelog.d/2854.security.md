- **Per-person sprint capacity is now a governed team signal (api, web)**: `GET /sprints/{id}/capacity/` returned every assigned member's name with committed and available hours to any Viewer. It is now the `capacity` signal under ADR-0104, default `TEAM`/`TEAM`: a reader above the audience (the PM band by default) gets `members: []` and `capacity_suppressed: true` while the aggregate `totals` stay. The Capacity preflight panel shows a team-private note. A new completeness test fails when a serializer carrying a per-person identity is not registered to a governed signal.
