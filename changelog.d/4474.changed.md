The WASM scheduler's JS entry points now throw a structured `SchedulerError` instead of a bare string. It carries a stable `kind` (`parse`, `invalid_input`, `cycle`, `unknown_task` or `schedule`), the `message`, and, for a cycle, the full `cycle` chain the Python engine reports on `CyclicDependencyError.cycle`. The crate is also marked `publish = false` until the publishing decision is made, so an accidental `cargo publish` cannot freeze today's API.
