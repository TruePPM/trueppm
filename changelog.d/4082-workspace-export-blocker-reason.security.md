The full-workspace export archive no longer includes the private free-text blocker reason. `projects/tasks.json` and `history/tasks.json` now omit the `blocked_reason` column, because that text is readable only by the task's assignee or a user @-mentioned on the task, not by the workspace Admin or Owner who runs the export. Each blocked task's type, age, "waiting on" link, and who flagged it are still exported.
