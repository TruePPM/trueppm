Shared chart layer (`features/charts/`) on the Pass 1 tokens: an S-curve with a forecast band, marker rules and a today line that renders straight from the metric-provenance forecast payload, a stepped (never spline) burndown/burn-up, a risk-zone band, and the heat palette (`--p1-heat-1..5` plus the existing over-allocation pair). Marker labels are row-packed so no line is drawn through a label, every chart carries a hover/focus/touch definition and a data table, and marks repaint with system colors under forced-colors. A public specimen sheet at `/design/charts` shows each chart at its sample data.
