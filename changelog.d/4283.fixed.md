- **Decisions-view oversight visibility now syncs live**: changing the project
  Decisions view's oversight-visibility consent switch previously saved with no
  WebSocket broadcast, so a peer with the Decisions panel open rendered a stale
  posture until their next full refetch — and a denied oversight reader whose
  decisions list had already settled into its locked 403 state stayed locked
  indefinitely even after an admin opted them in. The toggle now broadcasts
  `decisions_policy_changed`, deferred until the write commits, and the web app
  live-invalidates both the policy query and the decisions list on receipt, so a
  reader unlocks (or re-locks) without a manual reload.
