- **Monte Carlo is faster on very large projects (scheduler)**: the forward pass re-walks every task once per chunk of runs, so chunking over runs (#3824) cost fixed per-task call overhead that grew with the chunk count. The chunk budget is now 8M cells (was 4M): 50,000 tasks x 1,000 runs takes ~5.7 s (was ~6.5 s; ~4.9 s before chunking) at ~918 MB peak RSS (was ~790 MB; ~2,088 MB before chunking). 10,000 tasks x 1,000 runs was never slower (~1.0 s). Seeded results are unchanged. Recovering the rest of the time needs a cheaper per-task loop, not a larger chunk.
