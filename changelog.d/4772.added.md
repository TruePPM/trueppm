Programs: a decision log for the calls a program is waiting on. Each decision has an owner, a due date, a status (awaiting, made, withdrawn) and the owner's own impact estimate, which is always labeled "Owner's estimate · as of <date>" and never shown as a forecast. Program members read it, Team Members and up log and update decisions, and Program Managers and up delete them. A decision can link to one task or risk in the same program. New endpoints are under `/api/v1/programs/{id}/decisions/`, and the page is at **More → Decisions** in the shell v2 preview.
