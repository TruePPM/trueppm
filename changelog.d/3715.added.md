Your View focus now suggests which views to keep out of a project's tabs the first time you open a project you join or open after upgrading (PM hides the product backlog; Scrum Master hides Schedule, Calendar and Team); projects you had already opened keep their tabs. Suggestions are per project, labeled, and reversible, and switching focus never re-hides anything. The server resolves each view's visibility with a reason (`source`) and a `binding` at `GET /api/v1/projects/{id}/views/`, with `PATCH`, `apply-seed/` and `reset/` for your own state; the write routes declare their `400 {code: last_plan_layout}` refusal in the OpenAPI schema. The new `get_project_views` MCP read tool explains why a view is hidden, and it previews a project's suggestions without saving them, so an `mcp:read` agent never sets your view set (ADR-1478).
