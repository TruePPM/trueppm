Your View focus now suggests which views to keep out of a project's tabs the first time you open it (PM hides the product backlog; Scrum Master hides Schedule, Calendar and Team). Suggestions are per project, labeled, and reversible, and switching focus never re-hides anything. The server resolves each view's visibility with a reason (`source`) and a `binding` at `GET /api/v1/projects/{id}/views/`, with `PATCH`, `apply-seed/` and `reset/` for your own state, and the new `get_project_views` MCP read tool explains why a view is hidden (ADR-1478).
