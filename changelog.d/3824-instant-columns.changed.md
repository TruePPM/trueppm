- **Monte Carlo holds milestone instants for milestone columns only (scheduler)**: the forward pass kept each run's milestone instant (a date ordinal, a start-of-day flag and a working-time deficit) as three more runs × tasks matrices, 13 of its 29 bytes a cell, although only milestone and zero-duration-with-estimate columns ever write or read them. They are now held for those columns alone. Peak memory at 50,000 tasks × 1,000 runs drops from ~590 MB to ~475 MB RSS (~890 MB to ~785 MB where the sampled-duration matrix is held), and a 5,000-task × 1,000-run project with 2% milestones, the largest the API's default caps admit, from ~261 MB to ~238 MB. Results are unchanged: seeded P50/P80/P95, the distribution and the sensitivity tornado are byte-identical, checked by SHA-256 of the full result over a 400-project seeded fuzz corpus and the benchmark shapes. Durations stay float64.
