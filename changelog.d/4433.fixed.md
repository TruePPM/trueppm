- **Closing a milestone no longer slips the schedule by a day**: marking a milestone
  complete records its `actual_finish`, and the scheduler then treated it as ordinary
  work ending at the end of that day — so every FS successor, and the project finish,
  moved one working day later than the same milestone live or completed without
  actuals. A completed milestone with recorded actuals now stays an instant at the
  start of its `actual_finish` (else `actual_start`) day in both the Python engine
  and the Rust/WASM engine, and Monte Carlo inherits the same placement. The API's
  reading of which edge of the day a finish falls on agrees, so a project ending on
  a closed milestone no longer reads a phantom extra working day in its finish,
  baseline capture, slip checks or milestone notifications (#4433).
