Scheduler: a milestone at the start of a day whose float runs to a non-working midnight (weekends and holidays, reached through a calendar-day lag, the data date or a non-working `actual_start`) now shows its late start on the next working day after that midnight (the Monday, across a weekend) instead of the working day before it, so its `total_float` is the working-day span from its early start to its late start, as it is for every other task. Previously such a milestone reported one working day more float than its shown dates. Both the Python and WASM engines changed, and the milestone's `total_float` is unchanged. That late start is the latest the milestone can start in working time *when the milestone itself is held there*. When its predecessor is what places it on that day, the project finish can still move: in `P -SS-> M -FS+3cd-> S`, with a base finish of Tue 2027-03-16, holding M on Mon 03-15 with `planned_start` leaves the finish where it is, but holding P on Mon 03-15 so that the SS link puts M at Mon 00:00 moves S to Thu 03-18 and the finish by two working days. Whether lag counts calendar or working days is a separate open decision (#2535) that this fix does not settle. After upgrading, the first recompute of an affected project moves the `late_start` / `late_finish` of those milestones, so it can write a `cpm_recalculated` activity event for each one, count them in that event's `recalc_moved_count`, and raise the rolled-up `late_start` of a summary task whose earliest late start was one of them.
