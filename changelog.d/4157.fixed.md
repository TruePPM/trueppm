- **Scheduler derivations cite the finish the engine actually used**: when a zero-duration milestone anywhere in the project ended it at an instant other than the end of the finish day — for example an unlinked milestone with a Saturday start-no-earlier-than date — `derive_value` explained an ordinary task's late start and late finish from a `project_finish` date the engine never seeded from, and blamed a duration pullback that never ran. The reported date was right; the explanation was wrong. Derivations now seed their backward pass from the same finish instant as the engine.
