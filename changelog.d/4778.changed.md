- **Metric provenance for My Work and sprint burn**: the My Work ship-date
  forecast (`/me/work` `signals.forecast`) now reads each project's latest Monte
  Carlo result the way the project's own forecast does, starting with a run a
  Member started that was not saved to history. Before this, the card could show a
  different P80 date from the project's forecast for up to a day. Its `as_of` is the
  driving run's `last_run_at`, and it carries a `definitions` sentence. The sprint
  burndown (`GET /sprints/{id}/burndown/`) is the one owner of the sprint burn
  series, commitment, pace, trend and projected finish (ADR-1314 amendment). It now
  returns `as_of`, `definitions` and `burn_status_reason` (`no_commitment` or
  `no_snapshots` when `burn_status` is `no_data`). The My Work sprint card carries
  the same fields, and the active-sprints card computes its trend with the same
  formula. The Metric vocabulary page lists the new figures and explains how a
  sprint's burn differs from the project's burn.
