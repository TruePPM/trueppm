`hidden_views` on `/api/v1/auth/me/` and `/api/v1/auth/me/profile/` is deprecated (`Deprecation` and `Sunset` headers, `deprecated: true` in the schema) and is removed in the release after 0.5. Reads now return only the views you hid yourself, across your projects. Writes apply to every project you belong to, and a project you have not opened yet still gets your View focus suggestions the first time you open it. Existing hidden views were copied into each of your projects as your own choices, so no view reappears.
