- **Monte Carlo no longer charges a full working day to a zero-duration task with a small estimate**: a 0-day task carrying a three-point estimate such as `(0, 0, 1)`, mean 1/6 day and mode 0, was floored at one working day in every run, so P50 moved one working day past the CPM finish even though most runs sampled almost nothing. Milestones arriving with PERT fields, for example from an MS Project import, each added a day of guaranteed slip. A draw that rounds away is now the same instant `schedule()` places, including how its links and lags measure, and a larger draw is work starting no earlier than that instant. **Seeded Monte Carlo results for zero-duration tasks with PERT estimates change**; tasks with a duration of one day or more are unaffected, as are zero-duration tasks without estimates.
