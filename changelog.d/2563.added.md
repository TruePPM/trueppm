Commitment-slip gate: a date change that moves committed sprint work past the active sprint end now applies only for someone who can accept scope changes (project Admin or above, or the team's Product Owner or Scrum Master), with a reason. Anyone else gets a pending slip that one of those people accepts or rejects, per row or per batch, and accept/reject reasons appear in the Decisions view. Pending slips are decided from the sprint's scope-change drawer, which the sprint panel badge now opens when a slip is waiting. Re-estimating work already in progress counts only its remaining days (ADR-1485 D1).
