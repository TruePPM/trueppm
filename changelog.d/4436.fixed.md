Total float no longer comes up short beside a milestone that ends the project at the start of a day when tasks use different calendars. A task on a longer working week than the finishing milestone's (for example a seven-day task beside a Monday-to-Friday milestone shown at the start of Monday) could finish on the Sunday before without moving the finish, but was reported a day short of float and could be marked critical early; a milestone on a different week from the finisher's had the same error in both directions. Both the Python and the Rust/WASM engines are fixed.
