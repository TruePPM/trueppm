The interactive read-only demo's published account now lands on the demo-ready
schedule instead of an empty My Work (#4151, ADR-1197). While
`TRUEPPM_DEMO_READ_ONLY` is on, `resolve_landing` sends the one published account
straight to its landing project's Schedule — the project `#4050`'s demo overlay
spent a seed pass making healthy — falling back to the ordinary role policy
whenever that project is missing, archived, soft-deleted, or not readable by the
account. A saved `default_landing` preference still wins over this, as it does
over every other landing rule. The demo's scheduled reset also moves from every 6
hours to once a day: `demo.reset.schedule` defaults to `"0 8 * * *"`, and the
`TruePPMDemoResetStale` / `TruePPMDemoResetNeverSucceeded` alert thresholds are
raised (to 26h) so they do not fire against the new cadence.
