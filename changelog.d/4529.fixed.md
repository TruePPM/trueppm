Monte Carlo now lays a velocity-sampled story out over the same sprints of calendar time on any per-task calendar: two 10-day sprints on a Mon/Wed/Fri task calendar end where they end on the project calendar (25 calendar days), instead of being re-expanded as 20 Mon/Wed/Fri days (44). A what-if (`perturb_task_duration`) on such a story converts its day offset into story points at the pace of the task's own calendar, so +18 Mon/Wed/Fri days moves the forecast by 18 Mon/Wed/Fri days (six weeks) instead of four weeks.
