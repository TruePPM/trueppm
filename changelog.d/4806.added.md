- **Allocation conflict check before save.** `POST /api/v1/allocations/` books a person on a project as a weekly share, and `?dry_run=1` returns the first week that would exceed their own ceiling, what adds up to it, and what shortening or moving the booking would leave. A booking over the ceiling is saved only with a reason, and the person is notified. The check follows the people load view's visibility rules: it covers people on a roster you schedule, and load on a project whose per-person load you cannot read counts toward the verdict but is never shown as a number.
