- **Monte Carlo reports when the velocity horizon clamped a run**: a sprint-delivered task's velocity draw that has not burned down by the sprint horizon is clamped to it, which makes that run's duration a lower bound. The scheduler's `MonteCarloResult` now carries `velocity_saturated_runs` (and `velocity_tail_truncated`), and the backlog forecast response (`GET /api/v1/projects/{id}/sprint-forecast/`) carries `horizon_saturated`, so a caller can tell a truncated P80/P95 from an ordinary estimate. Seeded percentiles are unchanged. The entry for this gap is removed from Known Issues.
