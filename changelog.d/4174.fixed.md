- **Scheduler: work before a project-ending milestone keeps its float**: when a milestone that ends the project was reached through a calendar-day lag (for example `A -FS+1d-> M` landing on a Saturday), its predecessor was reported with zero total float and put on the critical path, even though slipping it a day did not move the finish in working time (the milestone's shown date may hop from Friday's end to Monday's start, which is the same point). The milestone's latest position now covers every midnight up to the next working day, in both the Python and WASM engines, so the predecessor keeps its day of float, as it does when another task follows the milestone. This regression was introduced during 0.4 development and was never in a tagged release (#4174).
