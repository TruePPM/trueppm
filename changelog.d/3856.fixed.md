- **The `trueppm-scheduler` sdist's test suite runs**: `tests/test_wasm_conformance.py` asserted at import that the monorepo's `packages/wasm-scheduler/fixtures` tree exists, which the sdist does not ship, so `pytest` in an unpacked sdist stopped at collection and ran zero tests. The module now skips when the fixtures are absent, and the rest of the suite runs. Inside a monorepo checkout (detected from its layout, or asserted by `TRUEPPM_MONOREPO=1` in CI and `make test-scheduler`) a missing fixture is still a hard failure (#1506) (#3856).
