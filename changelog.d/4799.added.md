The new Program Overview (`?ff=shell_v2`) gains supporting tiles (Projects on track, Cross-project links, Open risks, Over-allocated) with definitions and an as-of time, and a one-line explanation for figures it cannot compute (Program budget burn). `GET /programs/{id}/rollup/` now returns `summary` (counts over the projects the caller is a member of, with `projects_in_scope_count` as the denominator) and matching `definitions`, and `kpis.cost_variance` / `kpis.budget_utilization` carry `projects_without_budget_ids` for cost-gated callers. Over-allocated reads `GET /me/people-load/?program=`.
