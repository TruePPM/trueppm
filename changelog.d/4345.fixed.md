- **Scheduler: a milestone's predecessors no longer lose a day of free float to
  a successor that started on a non-working day (#4345).** Free float read a work
  successor's Saturday `actual_start` at the Monday after it (#4332), but the
  bound on how far a milestone may move before that successor moves still read
  the raw Saturday, so every predecessor of the milestone reported one working
  day less `free_float` than it can actually slip. Both now read the same
  working-day start, in the Python engine and the Rust/WASM engine alike.
