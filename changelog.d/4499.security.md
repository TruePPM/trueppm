A password reset, member off-boarding, and `revoke_api_tokens --user` / `--all-personal` now stamp a per-account credential cutoff, and every access token, refresh token, and WebSocket ticket issued at or before it is refused with a `401`. Before this, a stolen access token stayed valid for the rest of its 15-minute lifetime after a reset. In that window it could mint a WebSocket ticket and open a new socket after the reset's eviction had already run, or mint a personal token, project token, or share link that outlived the reset. The check lives in the JWT authenticator, so it covers every endpoint, and it adds no query per request. The comparison is strict to the second, so a sign-in that completes in the same second as the reset has to sign in again.
