Three sync-protocol fixes. A `since` value or pagination cursor past the 64-bit integer range now returns `400` instead of `500` on both sync pulls. A version-only "touch" (`save(update_fields=["server_version"])`) now records its sync cursor, so the change reaches the next delta pull. A save whose `update_fields` names only `last_sync_version` or `tombstone_floor`, which `save()` never writes, now raises `ValueError` instead of silently dropping the write, and on models that stamp no field of their own, `update_fields=[]` no longer moves the version or sync counters without a write. Clearing a retro action item's link when its promoted task is deleted, singly or through a bulk task delete, now reaches offline clients on the next delta pull.
