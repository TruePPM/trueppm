- **Monte Carlo's handling of in-progress estimated tasks is documented and pinned (scheduler)**: an in-progress task's remaining work in the simulation is its sampled total less the elapsed working days of its *planned* duration, the same elapsed `schedule()` counts. That rule is now stated in `monte_carlo()`'s docstring and in ADR-0132, and pinned by a fixed-sample oracle test. The early-biased truncation once reported here can no longer occur, because every sampled duration is floored at the planned duration first. Seeded results are unchanged.
