- **Scheduler: work before a milestone shown at the start of a day keeps its free float**: when a lag places a milestone on a midnight shown as the start of the next working day (for example `A(4d) -FS+2d-> M` placing it at Sunday midnight, shown on Monday), its predecessor was reported with zero free float, and the link was reported as driving. Slipping the predecessor a day moves the milestone only to Monday midnight, which is still shown on Monday. In both the Python and WASM engines, free float now measures a milestone successor by the day it is shown on. The predecessor keeps its day of free float, and the link no longer drives. A milestone shown at the end of a day (`A -FS+1d-> M`, shown on Friday) is unchanged. This regression was introduced during 0.4 development and was never in a tagged release (#4180).
