- A change to a sprint's commitment now notifies the people who can accept it. `sprint.scope_change_requested` and `sprint.commitment_at_risk` reach everyone with the scope accept authority (Admin and up, Scrum Master and Product Owner), and one drag or bulk placement makes one batched notice ("3 tasks · accept all / review"). The in-app notice cannot be muted: the preference cell stays on, the settings refuse to turn it off, and the inbox no longer offers "Mute notifications like this" for them. Email still follows each member's preferences.
