Notes

Q8.4 answer

A PM on a phone can decide but can't restructure. Pass 3 kept rebaselining desktop-only, and its phone Baselines frame was read-only. That changes here. The decision (read the changeset and its reasons, then take v2 with a reason or keep v1) is small, time-sensitive and well suited to a phone, for example in the hallway after the customer agrees a date. Structural editing stays on desktop.

Desktop-only vs phone

Part of section 3Phone
3A Commit plan (draft to v1)Desktop-only. The first commit reviews the whole plan.
3B Add, move or deleteDesktop-only. The phone outline has no structural controls, and 4A-a says so.
3B Answer a missing reasonOn the phone, inline in the review sheet. Unreasoned changes are listed first (D11).
3B Non-structural editsOn the phone: status, % complete and dates (as today).
3B Open-changeset indicatorOn the phone, as a card at the top of Plan (4A-a).
3C Review: list, reasons, net effectOn the phone as a full-height sheet (4A-b), one card per change.
3C Take v2 / Keep v1On the phone: two equal buttons in a sticky footer (4A-b, 4A-c). Take v2 takes the #4813 reason code, the free text and the commitment tick (off by default), and stays disabled while any change lacks a reason.
3D Change-control trailOn the phone, read-only, through More › Baselines (the Pass 3 phone frame).

Pins

1The changeset card sits in the content, not the chrome, so the phone keeps its rows. It shows the count, since when, the finish against v1 with “as of”, and one action.
2There is no dead end. The note says what is desktop-only, what the phone can still do, and gives two ways to hand off: Copy link, and Email it to me. That email goes to the signed-in user's own address and nowhere else.
3The two exits are equal buttons, with neither primary. “Take v2…” opens the reason step. “Keep v1” takes one tap, followed by an Undo toast that lasts 10 s. The extra step on v2 is the product commitment, not an imbalance in weight.

Open questions

Q4.1Should Keep v1 on the phone take a note? I made it one tap with Undo. Desktop offers an optional note. Should the phone match, at the cost of a step?
Q4.2Is “Email it to me” worth building, or is Copy link enough? It relies on the existing notification email, so it may be free.
Q4.3Can the phone take v2 while desktop edits are mid-flight (another tab has the reason sheet open)? I propose that the server rejects a stale v2 when the changeset changed after the sheet opened (409 with the new count), and the sheet reloads.
Q4.4Should push fire for “3 changes open” after N days? Not drawn. It overlaps Pass 4 · §4 preferences.
