# Rule 390 — A teaching surface's glyph is a claim about a control, and a removal justified by a replacement lands WITH …

> **Invariant.** Indexed from [`packages/web/CLAUDE.md`](../../../packages/web/CLAUDE.md), section *Controls and composition*. The index carries the rule's headline; this file carries its full text (#3744, ADR-0653 amendment). Binding everywhere, not only on the surface that produced it.

**A teaching surface's glyph is a claim about a control, and a removal justified by a replacement lands WITH the replacement — never on the promise of it (#3257).** The coach bar taught the row cluster as `+ ⇤ ⇥ ◆`; #3115 unpinned the toolbar's Milestone button *because* the row's ◆ replaced it; the row rendered two buttons. So the act was taught on one surface, demoted from another, and absent from the surface it was taught on — for weeks, on the one surface whose entire justification is teaching — and `scheduleTeachingChords.test.tsx` licensed the line throughout, because its guard asked only whether the glyphs were keystroke traps: it checked them for what they were *not* (keys) and never for what they *were* (controls). Three invariants: (a) **a control removed on the strength of a replacement ships in the same MR as the replacement, or the teaching surface changes in the removal MR** — an issue's "depends on X" is not a gate (nothing reads it), and the removal half is always the half that lands first. (b) **Every glyph a teaching surface prints resolves to a rendered control, derived from the render rather than from a list** — the control carries `data-glyph`, the guard renders an authorable row and reads the set back, exactly the shape of the `<kbd>`→bindings check, because a list of expected glyphs rots the way the coach bar did. A control whose mark is a house SVG (rule 242) is why the attribute exists: its `textContent` is empty, so the glyph has to be declared. The cluster's size is one constant (`NUDGE_COUNT`), so a fourth control changes the lane, the bar's line, and the guard together or fails. (c) **A soft-disabled row control routes its click to the act that owns the refusal** rather than restating the sentence — `convertToMilestone` speaks `MILESTONE_REFUSES_SUMMARY` from the row menu, the outline twin and the ◆ alike, so there is one sentence and every route to the act says it (rule 387 governs the control's readable styling; this governs where the words live). Reference: `features/schedule/TaskListRow.tsx` (`RowStructureNudges`), `buildMode/ScheduleCoachBar.tsx`, `scheduleTeachingChords.test.tsx`, `scheduleConstants.ts`.
