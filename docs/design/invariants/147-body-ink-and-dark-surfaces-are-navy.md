# Rule 147 — Body ink and dark surfaces are navy

> **Invariant.** Indexed from [`packages/web/CLAUDE.md`](../../../packages/web/CLAUDE.md), section *Color and tokens*. The index carries the rule's headline; this file carries its full text (#3744, ADR-0653 amendment). Binding everywhere, not only on the surface that produced it.

**Body ink and dark surfaces are navy.** `--neutral-text-primary` = navy `#1B2A4A` (light) / reversed `#E9EDF3` (dark). Dark surfaces are navy: `--neutral-surface` #15223C, `--chrome-surface` #0E1626. (Canvas bar-label `COLOR.text` stays `#1A1917` — legibility-tuned for bar fills, rule 72; the divergence is intentional.)
