# Rule 335 — A phase swap that replaces a surface with its OUTCOME must announce the outcome, not just seat focus on a …

> **Invariant.** Indexed from [`packages/web/CLAUDE.md`](../../../packages/web/CLAUDE.md), section *Dialogs, focus and keyboard*. The index carries the rule's headline; this file carries its full text (#3744, ADR-0653 amendment). Binding everywhere, not only on the surface that produced it.

**A phase swap that replaces a surface with its OUTCOME must announce the outcome, not just seat focus on a button beside it — a live region inserted together with its own content is not a reliable announcement, and moving focus states the control's name and nothing else.** Rule 245 says re-seat focus when a dialog swaps phase; rule 288 says prefer the phase's primary action over a `tabIndex={-1}` status node. Follow both correctly and a screen-reader user lands on `Retry 1 story, button` having never heard *which* story failed or why — which on a reconciliation surface is the entire payload, since the count alone is the one part nobody can act on. The two mechanisms each have a hole and they do not cover each other: `role="status" aria-live="polite"` is specified to announce *mutations* of a region that was already in the accessibility tree, so a panel mounted with its text in the same commit is announced inconsistently across AT; and a scripted `.focus()` announces the accessible name of the thing focused, which is the button. Three obligations. **(a)** Give the outcome panel a stable `id` and point the seat target's `aria-describedby` at it, so the breakdown is read *with* the control the user was just handed — this works whether or not the live region fired, and it costs one attribute. **(b)** The described node is the **whole** breakdown, never the summary line alone: "37 of 40 committed" without the three names and their reasons is the withheld half. **(c)** The seat target carries a `focus:` ring, never `focus-visible:` (rule 288(c)) — it is reached by a *scripted* focus, which browsers may decline to treat as visible — and if it sits on a sage fill it takes the rule-4 navy-on-sage ring (`ring-navy-700` / `ring-offset-sage-500`), not `ring-brand-primary`. The general form: **focus placement and announcement are two separate obligations, and satisfying the first is what makes it look like you satisfied the second.** Reference: `features/sprints/StoryPickerModal.tsx` (`RESULT_PANEL_ID`, `CommitResultPanel`, the `resultActionRef` seat effect), `features/schedule/buildMode/bulkEdit/BulkEditSheet.tsx` (`ResultPhase`, the same shape) — #2914.
