# Rule 399 — A `::before`/`::after` overhang — a touch target, a hit-area cushion, a decorative bleed — is INVISIBLE to …

> **Invariant.** Indexed from [`packages/web/CLAUDE.md`](../../../packages/web/CLAUDE.md), section *Accessibility floors*. The index carries the rule's headline; this file carries its full text (#3744, ADR-0653 amendment). Binding everywhere, not only on the surface that produced it.

**A `::before`/`::after` overhang — a touch target, a hit-area cushion, a decorative bleed — is INVISIBLE to the eye and to `boundingBox()`, but it is real content for an ancestor's `scrollWidth`. "The control is inside the viewport" is therefore not the same assertion as "the container does not overflow", and a surface that asserts only the first is not guarded.** The account chip ends the shell bar and extends its tap area to the 44px floor with `max-md:before:-inset-[6px]` (rule 5/247). At 375px its *box* sat at 371.45px — comfortably inside the screen, passing the `boundingBox()` check the spec had — while the six pixels of overhang reached 377.45 and made the header report `scrollWidth - clientWidth = 2`. Nothing was clipped, nothing looked wrong, and the two assertions in the same test disagreed with each other for months (#3505). Four consequences. **(a) The overflow is attributed to the wrong thing.** Because the overhang is unnamed and unpaintable, a 2px header overflow reads as "the widest health-chip word is 2px too wide" and sends you shaving type off a chip that is not the problem — the real chain was a *shrink-0* region already 8.45px past its content box (the header's `px-3` absorbing it in silence), plus 6px of pseudo on top. **Measure the chain, do not attribute the residue**; the overhang is the messenger. **(b) Padding hides the first half of the failure and the pseudo reveals the second.** A container whose trailing padding is wider than the overflow reports nothing at all, so the symptom appears only once the two exceed the padding — which makes the defect look like it arrived with whatever change last widened the row. **(c) Assert the overhang directly, not the box.** `getComputedStyle(el, '::before').right` is negative exactly when the pseudo reaches outside; `rect.right + max(0, -that)` is the number the container actually has to accommodate, and a regression on it says "the tap area left the screen" instead of "something is 2px too wide". jsdom resolves none of this (rule 330(c)), so the browser owns the measurement and vitest owns the class shape. **(d) The trailing element of a horizontally-constrained nowrap row is the only place this bites** — an overhang on a leading or mid-row control lands in the container's own padding. When raising a control to the touch floor (rule 366), ask where in its row it sits, not only how wide it became. Reference: `features/shell/UserMenu.tsx` (`AvatarChip`), `features/shell/TopBar.tsx`, `e2e/mobile-chrome-clip.spec.ts` (both assertions, and the band sweep that stopped the guard from testing its own best case) — #3505; the sibling instance with no guard is `features/sprints/SprintBacklogTable.tsx`'s trailing remove-`×` at `max-md:before:-inset-[14px]`.
