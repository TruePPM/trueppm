# Rule 309 — The Schedule row's far-left edge is spoken for twice — a third row-level meaning goes at the indent origin, …

> **Invariant.** Indexed from [`packages/web/CLAUDE.md`](../../../packages/web/CLAUDE.md), section *Dialogs, focus and keyboard*. The index carries the rule's headline; this file carries its full text (#3744, ADR-0653 amendment). Binding everywhere, not only on the surface that produced it.

**The Schedule row's far-left edge is spoken for twice — a third row-level meaning goes at the indent origin, not there.** `border-l-2 border-brand-primary` on the row already encodes **selection**, and `ModeGutter`'s absolutely-positioned 3px stripe at `left-0` already encodes **delivery mode** — the two were deliberately stacked (one is the padding edge, one is just inside it) and there is no room for a third without making all three ambiguous. When a new row-level fact needs a visual edge, put it at the row's **indent origin** — `(level - 1) * WBS_INDENT + 8`, the same x the task cell pads to — where the row's depth is already stated. For containment specifically this is better than a compromise: a phase's band edge and its children's deepest depth guide then land on the *same* x, so the container's edge visually continues down through its contents and "inside that phase" is one line you can follow rather than two marks near each other. Three obligations. **(a)** Derive the offsets from `WBS_INDENT` and one shared helper, and pin the edge-equals-deepest-guide identity in a test — it is invisible to every other check, and a change to `WBS_INDENT` or to either formula silently breaks the design while everything still renders. **(b)** Overlay marks are `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events-none`, and **siblings** of the cell's content, never wrappers: containment is already announced by `aria-level`, the WBS number and the fold caret's `N inside` count, so the marks are redundant encodings (which is also why they may sit below the 3:1 non-text contrast floor — WCAG 1.4.11 governs marks that carry information alone), and a wrapper or a missing `pointer-events-none` reintroduces the #2782 class where a de-emphasis layer froze the task list. **(c)** Band on **phase**, not on `isSummary`: `isSummary` is true for a leaf whose only children are drawer subtasks (ADR-0060), and banding one says "container" about a row that contains no structural work. Prefer the server's `isPhase` and fall back to the structural-child test, so a deployment without #1753 does not silently lose the whole signal. Reference: `features/schedule/RowContainmentChrome.tsx`, `features/schedule/RowModeIndicators.tsx` (the stacking it works around) — #2956, from `design_handoff_trueppm_v4/` "Reading structure".
