# Rule 365 — A ground change that carries meaning must be measured against the ground it actually sits on — and a …

> **Invariant.** Indexed from [`packages/web/CLAUDE.md`](../../../packages/web/CLAUDE.md), section *Dialogs, focus and keyboard*. The index carries the rule's headline; this file carries its full text (#3744, ADR-0653 amendment). Binding everywhere, not only on the surface that produced it.

**A ground change that carries meaning must be measured against the ground it actually sits on — and a `-raised`/`-sunken` step only steps within its own token family.** `--chrome-*` and `--neutral-*` are two independent ramps (rule 8a), not one, so a cross-family "raised" is arithmetically a non-step: `neutral-surface-raised` (`#FAF8F3`) laid on the sidebar's `chrome-surface` (`#F8F7F3`) is **1.01:1** in light theme — no lift at all — while reading as a genuine lift in dark (1.27:1). That asymmetry is what makes the class dangerous: the defect is invisible to a dark-mode screenshot, and the light-mode reviewer sees a flat strip and assumes the token is simply subtle. When a container's *meaning* is carried by its ground — a pinned footer band, a raised toolbar, a sticky header, a selected segment (rule 179 is this rule for one control) — take the `-raised`/`-sunken` sibling of the token the **parent actually paints**, and state the measured light-mode and dark-mode ratios in the MR description. `chrome-surface-raised` on `chrome-surface` is 1.10:1 light / 1.14:1 dark: a smaller lift than the cross-family accident produced in dark, and the only one that exists in *both* themes. If the step lands under ~1.1:1 in either theme the ground is not the mechanism — add a real one, and do not fall back on a `neutral-border` hairline, which `globals.css` itself records as 1.30:1 as a sole boundary, well under WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1. Two corollaries. **A class-name assertion is not evidence that a surface lifts** — `expect(el.className).toContain('bg-neutral-surface-raised')` is green whether the token steps or not; it can only prove the token was typed, so pair it with the measured ratio in the review. **The focus ring's offset colour is part of the ground**, not part of the row: `focus:ring-offset-*` paints a 1px gap in a literal colour, so a row reused on a raised container needs the offset swapped with it or the halo shows a seam of the wrong surface. Reference: `features/shell/Sidebar.tsx` (`rowClass(active, onRaisedGround)`, the WORKSPACE scope band), ADR-0942 §11 — #3136.
