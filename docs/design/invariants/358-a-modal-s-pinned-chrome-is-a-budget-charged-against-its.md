# Rule 358 — A modal's PINNED chrome is a budget charged against its scroller, and a scripted mount-focus SCROLLS that …

> **Invariant.** Indexed from [`packages/web/CLAUDE.md`](../../../packages/web/CLAUDE.md), section *Surface states*. The index carries the rule's headline; this file carries its full text (#3744, ADR-0653 amendment). Binding everywhere, not only on the surface that produced it.

**A modal's PINNED chrome is a budget charged against its scroller, and a scripted mount-focus SCROLLS that scroller — so autofocusing a control below the fold silently pushes the surface's lead content off the top, at every viewport height the reviewer did not open.** `element.focus()` defaults to `preventScroll: false`, so a `ref.current?.focus()` in a mount effect scrolls every scrollable ancestor until the target is visible. That is invisible on a tall display and total on a short one. #3130 reordered `NewProjectModal` so the way-in cards lead the sheet, then moved the working calendar into the pinned footer — taking fixed chrome from ~136px to ~309px — while still autofocusing the Name input, now ~300px into the scroller. On a 1366x768 CSS viewport under `max-h-[90vh]` the cards the reorder exists to promote are scrolled entirely out of view **at rest**, before the user has touched anything. Three obligations. **(a) Budget the chrome.** Header + pinned footer must leave the scroller a usable height at the surface's *minimum* supported viewport — 640px tall for a `max-h-[Nvh]` desktop dialog, and the device height for any full-screen compact branch, which has no `vh` cushion and is therefore the TIGHTER constraint, not the looser one. Growing a pinned footer is not free; it is subtracted from the body at every height, and multi-line helper text under a pinned control is the usual culprit. **(b) Autofocus the top, or do not scroll.** Seat mount focus on the first tab stop, or pass `{ preventScroll: true }` when a lower control is genuinely the better target (it is, when that control is the only empty required field). Never let the resting scroll position be decided by a focus call. **(c) Verify at the short viewport and in the compact branch, not at your own display.** A screenshot at 1080p passes on both failure modes; the check is opening the surface at the shortest viewport it claims to support and confirming its first meaningful content is on screen before any input. This is the height-axis twin of rule 316(c) ("never made conditional on the viewport") — same class, measured down instead of across. Reference: `features/shell/NewProjectModal.tsx`, `features/shell/useStartSheetCompact.ts`.
