# Rule 22b — MC chips use a colon separator

> **Decision record.** Moved out of `packages/web/CLAUDE.md` by #2433 (ADR-0653): precedent bound to one surface, not a general invariant. It is still binding for the surface it governs.
>
> Original section: *Monte Carlo Row Rules*

**MC chips use a colon separator** — `P50: Nov 30`, `P80: Nov 30`, `P95: Nov 30`. Consistent across `MonteCarloTimeline` and `MobileMonteCarloCard`. Never render the label and date with only a space. **Every forecast surface derives its chips from `useForecastPresentation` (#2426) — no surface computes its own state or writes its own copy.** That is exactly how the Schedule chip row came to render `[P50: Oct 6] [P80: Oct 6 (+11d)] [P95: Oct 6]` while the Overview tile said, correctly, "Every simulation finished on October 6, 2026. No date spread to plot" — two surfaces contradicting each other about the same run. Three invariants: (a) **a degenerate run (`p50 === p80 === p95`, the same equality `MonteCarloHistogram` already uses) collapses to a SINGLE chip and the percentile keys are suppressed, not zeroed** — `P80` on a single-value result is a claim about a distribution that does not exist; (b) **a delta never renders without its baseline date on screen** — inline in the solo chip (`Forecast: Oct 6 · +11d vs CPM (Sep 25)`), or as a trailing **dashed** reference chip beside the three percentile chips on a real spread, and on mobile the baseline stays a chip in the stack, never a tooltip (there is no hover); a zero delta says `matches CPM` in words rather than `+0d`; with no known CPM finish the delta is dropped entirely rather than shown unanchored; (c) **the degenerate-run note defers its *reason* to the server diagnostic** (`forecastFlatGuidance(result.forecastDiagnostic)`, #1340) — a flat forecast is not always "no estimates", it may be estimates pending approval, agile work with no velocity history, or estimated work off the critical path, so the copy must never hardcode a cause. Reference: `features/schedule/useForecastPresentation.ts`, `ScheduleForecastBar.tsx`, `MobileMonteCarloCard.tsx`, `lib/forecastFlatMessage.ts`.
