# Rule 223 — A co-located "velocity proves the schedule" card renders the deterministic date and the velocity estimate as two separately-labeled reads that never merge into one authority, and computes any "since last close" delta on the CPM finish only

> **Decision record.** Moved out of `packages/web/CLAUDE.md` by #2433 (ADR-0653): precedent bound to one surface, not a general invariant. It is still binding for the surface it governs.
>
> Original section: *Mobile bottom rail caps at 5 slots; overflow goes to a BottomSheet, never a scroller (Issue #1464)*

**A co-located "velocity proves the schedule" card renders the deterministic date and the velocity estimate as two separately-labeled reads that never merge into one authority, and computes any "since last close" delta on the CPM finish only.** When a CPM finish and a velocity forecast sit in the same card region (the `MilestoneBridgeForecast` bridge, #730, ADR-0241), the exact CPM date carries **no** estimate qualifier (it is deterministic), while the velocity read **always** carries its basis marker — `est. {p50}–{p80}` + an on-screen `(velocity estimate)` qualifier when `basis === 'velocity_band'`, and `P80 {date}` **only** when `basis === 'monte_carlo'` (extends rule 166 to the two-column co-located form). The "changed since last {iteration}" delta chip is derived from `cpm_finish` movement **only** — never from the velocity band, which is noisy sprint-to-sprint — is hidden when the prior snapshot is absent or unchanged, and states its direction in words (`+3d later` / `-2d earlier`, `→` `aria-hidden`) so polarity never rides on tone color alone (rules 120/169). Muted supporting text (qualifier, `since …` attribution, projection) uses `neutral-text-secondary`, never `neutral-text-disabled` (rule 169). Reference: `features/sprints/MilestoneBridgeForecast.tsx`.
