# Rule 234 — On the schedule PDF report, HUE encodes the worst risk band and TEXTURE/SHAPE encodes the redundant grayscale-safe signal — and the risk band lives on the bar's BORDER, never its fill (which is progress)

> **Decision record.** Moved out of `packages/web/CLAUDE.md` by #2433 (ADR-0653): precedent bound to one surface, not a general invariant. It is still binding for the surface it governs.
>
> Original section: *Schedule PDF report — risk on the border, shape-encoded states, expanded legend (Issue #1686, ADR-0277)*

**On the schedule PDF report, HUE encodes the worst risk band and TEXTURE/SHAPE encodes the redundant grayscale-safe signal — and the risk band lives on the bar's BORDER, never its fill (which is progress).** The export is a static, `html-to-image`-rasterized light document (no hover, no interactivity), so every state must be self-evident on paper and survive grayscale + deutan/protan (WCAG 1.4.1). The firewall (ADR-0277, supersedes the fill-by-risk model):
 - **Risk = border, progress = fill.** `barBorderClass(band)` frames the bar (critical = 2px `semantic-critical`, at-risk = 2px `semantic-at-risk`, on-track = 1px `neutral-text-secondary` hairline — the fainter `neutral-border` fails WCAG 1.4.11 for a bar that is still data); the interior keeps the green `semantic-on-track` progress fill. This is because the old model painted the green progress fill *over* the risk fill, so a 100%-complete critical task rendered solid green and the red critical base was fully masked (the "bars all green despite 18 critical tasks" bug). A completed critical task must read as a green bar in a **red frame**. **Never** put the risk band back on the fill. The 2px critical border clears WCAG 1.4.11 vs the white sheet; critical is additionally backed non-color by the row-label red dot + the Critical-Path-Chain list (do not remove either — they are the 1.4.1 redundancy the thin red-vs-green interior boundary, ~1.6:1, relies on).
 - **"Behind schedule" = a diagonal hatch overlay**, not a color. `hatchBackgroundStyle()` is a `repeating-linear-gradient` `background-image` (a `<div>` background, so it rasterizes reliably — rule 232 only bites SVG class-strokes) composed over *any* border, so a critical-and-behind bar reads red-frame + hatch. Keep `isBehind` as its own `SchedulePrintRow` flag: a critical bar's `riskBand` is `critical` (precedence), which would otherwise hide that it is also slipping.
 - **Overdue = shape, never a fourth hue** (red is exhausted by critical). A task past its finish and not complete (`isRowOverdue`) gets a red past-due flag polygon at its finish edge + a dashed red overrun tail to the data date (inline-`style` stroke/fill CSS-vars, rule 232). A pending milestone past its date is a **hollow red-outlined** diamond + a `!` glyph. Met milestones are never overdue.
 - **Milestone met vs pending = filled vs hollow diamond** (`milestoneDiamondClasses(met, overdue)`), a shape cue — resolves the #1686/#1683 color-only-amber gap. Never distinguish them by hue alone. **Both diamonds are outlined in navy `neutral-text-primary`, never amber:** the static `brand-accent` (#E8A020) is only ~2.2:1 on the white sheet — below the WCAG 1.4.11 3:1 floor for a content-bearing mark — so it is the **fill only**, never the sole boundary of a mark. More generally, any content-bearing mark on the export sheet must clear ≥3:1 against white; pair a sub-3:1 hue (brand-accent amber) with a dark outline or use a darker token.
 - **Row leaders are faint round-dotted hairlines** (label→bar), made distinct from dashed *soft* dependency arrows on three axes: dot pattern (`0.5 4` round vs `3 2` square), weight/color (`neutral-border` 0.75px vs `neutral-text-secondary` 1px), and zone (left gutter, horizontal, per row vs bar→bar connectors). A leader that would be a stub (`< 12px`) is omitted.
 - **The legend is grouped Bars / Links / Markers**, each swatch rendering its ACTUAL treatment (border/hatch/flag/filled-vs-hollow/dash) so the legend itself is grayscale-legible. Page 1 only, in the keep-together footer flow.
 - **Pagination reserves a bottom footer band** (`RESERVED_FOOTER_PT`, `exportSchedulePdf.ts`) so the "Page n of N" counter never collides with content; a centered **"continued on next page"** caption (plain ASCII — no `→` glyph, which jsPDF's standard font drops) + a hairline rule seal every non-final page. `scheduleVerticalPlan.ts` keeps the CP card whole when it fits a page and avoids stranding `< MIN_GANTT_ORPHAN_ROWS` (3) Gantt rows on a continuation page — a widow/orphan guard, **not** a rescale (the fixed print scale is deliberate; bars stay the same size on every page). Reference: `schedulePrintTheme.ts::{barBorderClass,hatchBackgroundStyle,milestoneDiamondClasses}`, `schedulePrintData.ts::{isRowOverdue,isBehind}`, `SchedulePrintLayout.tsx`, `scheduleVerticalPlan.ts`.
