# Rule 285 — A grid cell that is empty at rest sizes to its own content (self-start), and a board-wide count rendered above lane-scoped content names its scope (#2427)

> **Decision record.** Moved out of `packages/web/CLAUDE.md` by #2433 (ADR-0653): precedent bound to one surface, not a general invariant. It is still binding for the surface it governs.
>
> Original section: *AppShell owns the only `<main>`; the 404 keeps the shell (Issue #2184)*

**A grid cell that is empty at rest sizes to its own content (`self-start`), and a board-wide count rendered above lane-scoped content names its scope (#2427).** Two independent defects on the phase-grouped board, both about a value or a box implying something it does not mean. (a) **Empty cells stretched.** A swimlane is its own CSS grid, so its height is already driven by its own tallest column — the lane does *not* size to the board. But a grid cell stretches to its row by default, so in a lane made tall by one full column the empty siblings became full-height blank tracks with a lonely resting tick floating in the middle; on a project with several phases that pushes almost everything below the fold. The resting cell now carries `self-start` so it renders at its natural height and the remainder reads as lane background. **Only the resting cell shrinks** — as soon as a drag begins the full-height droppable cell renders, so a drop target is never smaller than the column it represents. Do not "simplify" this by giving the empty cell a fixed height: the drag-time cell must stay full-height. (b) **A board-wide count sat above lane-scoped content.** The column header row's counts are board-wide and correct for the board, but with swimlanes on they sit directly above lanes that each hold a share of them, so a bare `TO DO 8` above a lane showing no TO DO cards reads as a lane count that disagrees with its lane. The number is not wrong — its scope was unstated — so the header names the scope (`"TO DO, 8 tasks on the board"` in the accessible name, plus a `title` on the visible figure) whenever `laneCount > 1`, and stays in the plain form on an ungrouped board. Deleting the count or recomputing it per lane were the alternatives; naming the scope keeps a figure users rely on while removing the contradiction. WIP breach chips are unaffected and stay always-on (rule 176). Reference: `features/board/BoardView.tsx` (`showRestingTick`), `features/board/boardView/BoardColumnHeaderRow.tsx` (`headerAriaLabel`, `laneCount`).
