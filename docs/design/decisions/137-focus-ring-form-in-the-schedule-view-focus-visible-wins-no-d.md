# Rule 137 — Focus-ring form in the Schedule view: focus-visible: wins; no dark-mode override

> **Decision record.** Moved out of `packages/web/CLAUDE.md` by #2433 (ADR-0653): precedent bound to one surface, not a general invariant. It is still binding for the surface it governs.
>
> Original section: *Schedule Backlog-Promote Rules (Issue #318)*

**Focus-ring form in the Schedule view: `focus-visible:` wins; no dark-mode override.** The Schedule view (`features/schedule/`) uses `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-brand-primary focus-visible:ring-offset-1 focus-visible:ring-offset-neutral-surface` per rules 46/126 — this overrides the generic ux-review `focus:` preference for standalone controls. **Scope: this carve-out covers the schedule canvas/tree only — the Gantt engine (`features/schedule/engine/`) and the grid rows — NOT the task-detail drawer sections under `features/schedule/sections/`, which are ordinary standalone triggers and follow the standard rule-214 `focus:` treatment** (their action buttons were converted in #2166; do not "revert" them to `focus-visible:` in a future mechanical pass on the belief they are schedule-tree controls — the inline comments in those files guard against this locally). The Schedule view adapts to dark mode (rule 40), but `brand-primary` is now mode-aware sage (sage-700 #316F57 light / sage-400 #66B998 dark, ≥3:1 on the dark surface #15223C), so `ring-brand-primary` passes WCAG 1.4.11 in **both** modes. The old `dark:focus-visible:ring-semantic-on-track` override — added for the pre-rebrand green #1C6B3A's 2.81:1 dark failure — is **retired; do not reintroduce it** (rule 4, ADR-0103). Steppers inside a bordered `role="group"` may use `ring-inset` without an offset.
